Fixion / DRDR: the mobile UX proposal (speech, the action menu, the mic in the menu)

Written 2026-10-27 (18:30 JST). Built on docs/philosophy/PHILOSOPHY.md, ONTOLOGY.md and UX_IMPLICATIONS.md in the drdr-dr tree, on the audit in current_state.md (failure points F1 to F8) and on research.md. Phone portrait first, iPhone Safari, the HTML5 build of the DragonRuby game. Everything here is implementable with what DragonRuby 7.18 already does in HTML5: additive sprites, render targets, labels. No shaders.

Interactive mockups (touch and mouse, both skins, each state replayable): talk · menu · mic to action. Clips: ../media/. The implementation brief for the next round is fix_brief.md.

Josh's answers that shaped this (18:09 JST): show the player's own words in the feed by default (yes); the green indicator above the player while speaking comes back, in both skins; rewrites should be smooth; whether every spoken action waits for a confirm and whether a red reason is final are left to this proposal; a session is "for now just being in the scene".

Relation to the fix10 round (in progress at 18:25 JST). fix10.md (18:05 JST) was written from the same audit and is already building most of this: live partials, a connecting state, a speech pill over the mic, the slot with its phases, up/down-only selection with ✓/✕, a raised ACT, standing beams in the slot, a confirm policy, haptics. Where this proposal differs it says so; fix_brief.md lists the deltas for the round after fix10. The two disagreements that need Josh: the menu card's place (fix10 docks it low near the thumbs; this proposal keeps it beside the target) and whether a rewrite may auto-confirm (fix10: after a delay; this proposal: never).

1. The talk flow

Plain talking is invisible (Josh, after fix10; see the changelog). What the player says is never drawn on the game screen: no live transcript, no take card or pill, no speech bubble or caption over the @, no floating text, no stage labels. The words appear in exactly one place, as the ADVENTURER: … line in the feed. The only talk feedback is the green indicator over the @ while audio flows, and the mic button's own press state.

1a. States

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> idle
  idle --> arming: mic down (haptic#59; dashed ring on the mic button only)
  arming --> listening: audio flowing (AudioContext running, mic track live, socket configured)
  arming --> idle: mic up before audio flowed (nothing sent#59; the mic gives a tiny shake)
  arming --> blocked: getUserMedia denied
  listening --> finalizing: mic up (haptic#59; nothing on screen)
  finalizing --> sent: final text, non-empty (the line appears in the feed, ADVENTURER: …)
  finalizing --> idle: final text empty (nothing sent#59; the mic gives a tiny shake)
  finalizing --> idle: socket closed / 5 s cap (the take is retried from the buffered audio, see 1c)
  sent --> idle: the room answers after the 0.9 s floor
  blocked --> arming: mic down after the permission is granted

1b. What the player sees, state by state

idle. The mic as today: mint ring, the fixed button label "HOLD TO TALK".

arming (mic down). On the mic button only: the ring turns grey and dashed and rotates, the button is pressed in. Nothing over the @, no caption. The green must not come on until the browser reports an audio frame to the worklet and the Soniox socket has taken its config message (typical 60–150 ms on a warm page, 300–2,000 ms on a cold iOS page).

listening. The mic turns green with the three pulse rings scaled by level, as today. Over the @: five green bars (4 px wide, 4 px apart, 4–24 px tall by level, with the per-bar phase from the web build's drawVoice), an additive green glow behind them in the colour skin, |/:/. glyph columns in the ASCII skin. No words. This is the indicator Josh asked back for (18:09), in both skins, and it stays.

finalizing (mic up). The bars go, the mic returns to idle. Nothing else on screen: no card, no partial words, no dots. Median release-to-final is 2.3 s in the logs (F2); the "…" over the predicted responder is the next thing seen.

sent. The line ADVENTURER: … appears in the feed in the player's mint (the fix9 "Show my lines = Yes" default already draws it). That is the only place the player's words are ever shown. The "…" over the predicted responder appears as today and the floor runs.

Nothing sent (empty final, or released while arming). The mic button gives one tiny horizontal shake (240 ms) and a soft low tone. No text, no ? over the @, nothing in the feed.

blocked (permission denied). The one persistent state, because the player has to act on it: the mic button greys out with an amber slash and its label reads "MIC BLOCKED" until the permission is granted. Socket errors are not shown; they are retried (1c).

1c. Why invisible, and what "confirmation" means now

Josh's earlier wish for confirmation was about lost speech, not visible feedback: speech that was said must not silently vanish. That is an engineering guarantee, not a screen element. The bridge buffers audio from the moment of mic down (so the arming window does not eat the first words), never sends finalize to a socket that did not get its config, keeps the pre-opened socket for the next press, and retries a take whose socket closed from the buffered audio. When it works, the proof is the line in the feed and the room answering. The screen stays clean.

1d. Barge-in while talking

Pressing the mic during a performance cancels it as today. A press while the previous take is still finalizing starts arming at once; the previous take still lands in the feed when its final arrives; nothing is dropped.

2. The action menu

2a. Layout

  • The menu card stays beside the target on the map (right of it if there is room, else left, else below or above), as built in fix3. Reason: principle 2 of the UX implications, the scene stays visible; the thing you act on and the verbs are one glance apart. The alternative (a deck-docked list near the thumbs) was considered and rejected: it hides which target you are on and turns the map into a background.
  • Rows 46 px, 22 px type, the selected row mint with the › as today; » marks walk-up verbs; item rows keep their icons.
  • ACT is not lifted: it stays where it is today, level with the mic, size unchanged (r 56). (An earlier draft raised it 72 px; Josh undid that after fix10.) In Act mode the caption reads "LEAVE".

2b. Input

  • Stick: up/down only. Dead zone 16 px to take a direction, 10 px to let go (hysteresis, no jitter at the edge). A held direction repeats after 500 ms, then every 200 ms. Sideways motion in Act mode does nothing; the pad's left/right arrows are replaced by dots so the face says so.
  • ✕ / ✓ strip. 120 ms after the selection settles, a 40 px strip opens under the selected row (160 ms, ease-out; rows below move down in sync). ✕ red at the left of the pair, ✓ green at the right (Xbox positions, both as symbols, 38 px disks, 44 px hit area). A small "do it?" label at the left of the strip; for a slot with a rewrite it reads "do this instead?".
  • ✓ commits. Tapping the selected row again commits too (one-handed, no reach to the strip). Tapping any other row selects it and moves the strip.
  • ✕ closes the menu and leaves Act mode. ACT also leaves. The stick's left no longer means back.
  • Desktop: ↑↓, Enter, Esc, E unchanged.

2c. The commit moment (every pick, menu or slot)

ms what
0 the row washes white (alpha 0.55 → 0 over 420 ms), the strip closes, a 1800→600 Hz sine tick, haptic if it was a tap
0–90 hitstop: the menu and the deck freeze for 90 ms (the map keeps breathing)
0 a ·/. glyph ring expands from the row's centre (600 ms, ease-out), 9–12 glyph sparks (`- /
520 the △ line enters the feed; the walk-up (if any) starts on the map
900 the menu leaves (200 ms, ease-in) and Act mode ends

This is 글자들's contact() at power ≈ 1 (hitstop .05+.05, sparks, ring, a sound) applied to a UI commit. It is the same vocabulary the game already uses for a landed blow, so a menu pick feels like part of the world rather than a widget. Josh's "clear sign of being pressed" is the white wash plus the hitstop; the hitstop is what makes it read even under a thumb.

3. The mic in the menu (a spoken action)

3a. States

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> menu_idle: Act mode, a target, a selected row
  menu_idle --> slot_open: mic down (slot slides open at the bottom of the menu, selection jumps to it, 220 ms#59; no beam)
  slot_open --> slot_listening: audio flowing (same arming rule as talk)
  slot_listening --> slot_filling: mic up (the slot flashes once#59; heard words type into the slot, ≤ 400 ms)
  slot_filling --> slot_judging: words complete (underline sweeps#59; "judging…" after 2 s)
  slot_judging --> landed_auto: ok or duplicate, P ≥ 0.75, not hostile
  slot_judging --> landed_gated: rewrite, or P < 0.75, or hostile preset
  slot_judging --> reason: missing_prerequisite or meta
  slot_judging --> said: speech
  slot_judging --> landed_auto: sidecar error or 5 s timeout (fail open, "as said")
  landed_auto --> pressed: 320 ms after landing
  landed_gated --> pressed: ✓ (or tap the slot)
  landed_gated --> closed: ✕ / stick left
  pressed --> performed: 600 ms (△ line in the feed#59; the slot becomes a row with a violet dot)
  performed --> menu_idle_closing: 1.1 s, then the menu leaves
  reason --> slot_open: mic down again (the slot is replaced)
  reason --> closed: ✕ / stick
  said --> closed: the words drop into the feed as ADVENTURER:, Act mode leaves 400 ms later
  slot_listening --> nothing: mic up with no words ("didn't catch that", closes after 900 ms)
  slot_listening --> closed: ✕ mid-speech
  slot_judging --> closed: ✕ while judging (the sidecar's answer is discarded)

3b. Beat by beat

  1. Mic down (violet, in Act mode). A new row slides open at the very bottom of the menu, below every row: 0→46 px in 220 ms, ease-out, and the selection jumps to it. The slot is violet-tinted with a dashed border that marches. There is no beam or line from the mic to the slot (removed per Josh, 21:38 JST). The mic's ten orbiting sparks run as today. Over the @ nothing (an action is not speech).
  2. Speaking. Nothing is written. The slot shows "…" and its dashed border marches.
  3. Mic up. The slot flashes once, softly (its border and fill brighten violet and fade over 380 ms): the speech went through. The heard words type into the slot, 18 ms per character, capped at 400 ms for long phrases, with a blinking _. This is the one place the player reads what was heard before anything happens, and it answers F1.
  4. Judging. A violet underline sweeps under the words (900 ms loop). After 2 s of waiting a small "judging…" appears at the right of the slot; this covers the 2–5 s slow-network case without changing state. At 5 s the sidecar fails open: the words become the label with an "as said" tag and, if the preset is hostile, the gate still applies.
  5. Landed. The label arrives in the slot:
    • Kept (ok) or rewritten, the same motion: the heard words morph straight into the label with a 320 ms left-to-right churn (each heard letter turns into @#%&* glyphs and settles as the label's letter, the fix8 banner's motion) and a rising tone. No struck-through line is kept in the slot. A rewrite is gated (step 6), so the strip's "do this instead?" is what tells the player the words were changed. The feed gets the existing struck-through rewrite line at the perform step. The morph is the smoothness Josh asked for: no cut, the player's words are visibly becoming the label.
    • If it matched an existing verb (duplicate): the slot shows the existing label for 380 ms, then collapses while the existing row takes the press. No new row.
  6. Gate or not. ok/duplicate at P ≥ 0.75 and a non-hostile preset: the slot is pressed by itself 320 ms after landing. Anything else: the ✕/✓ strip opens under the slot and waits. Nothing is performed until ✓.
  7. Pressed, performed. Exactly the commit moment of §2c, from the slot. 600 ms later the △ line enters the feed and the slot turns into an ordinary row at the bottom of the menu (mint, » if it is a walk-up, a violet dot for 2.6 s that says "new"); learned verbs stay at the bottom of the list after that. The menu stays 1.1 s so the player sees the verb they made sitting among the others, then leaves. The verb is in the data already (fix3); now the player sees it join.
  8. Reason. The slot turns red-tinted: the one-sentence reason in bold red, "hold the mic to try again" at the right, the strip opens with "nothing happens" and ✕ only. The feed gets the red line as today. The slot stays: the next mic press replaces it in place (the retry costs one gesture and the player keeps their spot in the menu), ✕ or the stick closes it.
  9. Said. The slot shows the words in quotes with "said ↓" for 700 ms, then they drop into the feed as ADVENTURER: …, the slot closes and Act mode leaves 400 ms later; the room answers after the floor.

3c. Why gate only some of them

Every spoken action could wait for ✓. That would cost two gestures per action on the most common path and work against principle 9 (timing is most of the feel). The gate is placed exactly where principle 5 needs it: where the judge changed the player's words (a rewrite is a proposal and the player must see it before it is performed), where the judge was unsure (P < 0.75), and where the act hurts someone (the 양 case in the audit: "ok" at 0.83 and performed as a hit with blood). Hostile presets are hit, shoot, shove, grab (the sidecar's HOSTILE table) plus any learned verb whose preset maps to them. The sidecar already knows all three facts; api_inspire only has to return a gate field.

3d. Why a reason is not final

A reason is the judge saying "not with what you have". The slot keeping the mic live is the cheapest possible "then what?": the player is still on the same target with the same menu, and a second press replaces the red line. Making it final would send the player back through ACT → aim → mic for a one-word change ("set it on fire" → "wave it"). The feed copy is still written, so the history is kept.

5. Edge cases

  • Empty STT (talk). Nothing is sent and nothing is written: the mic button gives a tiny shake. Distinguished from a system failure (which is retried from the buffered audio) by whether the stream ever started (the audit's streamStarted test).
  • Empty STT (slot). The slot says "didn't catch that" for 900 ms and closes; the selection and strip return.
  • Cancel mid-speech (slot). ✕ or stick-left while listening: the slot closes, the socket's take is discarded (the bridge still finalizes the socket to stop billing, but the text is dropped).
  • Cancel while judging. Same; the sidecar's answer is ignored by id. Needs an inspire_id on the request and the game to ignore late answers (Player#inspired already checks @inspiring).
  • Barge-in while a slot is judging. A talk press is impossible in Act mode (the mic is the action mic), so there is no barge-in; ACT or ✕ cancels. Walking with the stick is impossible too (up/down only). Leaving Act mode with a slot judging cancels it.
  • The judge says impossible / forced outcome / multi-step / controls other. All land as rewrites and gate on ✓ with the heard words morphing into the rewrite. The impossible rewrite is the comedy case ("Attempt to fly"); it is shown before it is performed so the joke is shared, not discovered.
  • Slow network 2–5 s. The words stay in the slot under the sweeping underline; "judging…" after 2 s; at 5 s fail open to the words as said, still gated if hostile. The marching border and the underline carry the wait. Nothing else on screen moves.
  • The sidecar is down. As said, "as said" tag, no gate unless hostile (then ✓ is required, and the sidecar's absence means it performs locally and queues the facts).
  • Two takes in a row (talk). A press while the previous take is finalizing arms at once; the previous line still lands in the feed.
  • Scene switch mid-take. The bridge releases the mic and socket on scene switch (fix9 §15) and the state returns to idle; a take still finalizing is dropped (nothing was on screen, nothing reaches the feed).
  • Knocked out. Act mode is unavailable (today's rule); the talk flow is unchanged.
  • Korean worlds. Every caption has a Korean string (lang.rb); slot text and labels are Korean from the sidecar; the churn uses Hangul-safe glyphs (@#%&* are fine; no syllable scrambling).

6. VFX, within the HTML5 budget

  • No mic-to-slot beam (removed per Josh, 21:38 JST). The only "speech went through" effect is the slot's own one-shot flash: a filled rect and a 2 px frame on the slot, alpha fading over 380 ms. No extra sprites, no render target.
  • Churn. The fix8 join banner's glyph churn (skin_fx.rb), applied to one label.
  • Hitstop. Stage#time_scale already exists (0.35 for 42 ticks on a big moment). A menu hitstop is a 5-tick full stop applied to the UI layer only; the map's ambient animation keeps running so it reads as impact, not a hang.
  • Ring and sparks. The game's marks system (glyph particles with dx/dy/alpha) draws both; the ring is the 글자들 addRing · circle.
  • Flash. @flash_at exists; cap it at 14 % alpha for 2 frames.
  • No take card (talking is invisible). The mic's nothing-sent shake is a 240 ms x-offset on the button.
  • Haptics. A hidden <input type="checkbox" switch> in the page toggled on pointerdown/pointerup on the mic and on ✓; nothing on async results.
  • Sound. Synthesised like tools/synth_fx.py: tick (1800→600 Hz, 250 ms), arm (880 Hz, 40 ms), listen (1320 Hz), nothing sent (330 Hz triangle), reason (240 Hz square, 250 ms).

7. How PC differs (briefly)

  • The mic flow is identical with Space; talking is invisible there too, and the arming ring applies as it is (desktop browsers have their own first-press audio unlock).
  • The slot accepts typing: in Act mode, T or / opens the slot as a text field (the current dev path becomes the real one); Enter sends it to the same judge; the landing and gate are identical. This keeps one mental model for "a new verb" on both platforms.
  • The ✕/✓ strip still appears (mouse users click it); Enter/Esc do the same.
  • ACT's position does not matter on desktop; the landscape layout keeps its column.
  • The menu may dock in the right column on wide screens, since there is no thumb to reach; it then points at the target with the existing pointer line.

8. How each choice follows from the philosophy

  • P1 The screenplay is the world. The slot shows the words that become the △ line; plain talk becomes the ADVENTURER: line in the feed, the only place it is written. The player reads their input as script.
  • P2 The author never plays the player / P10 nothing shown that was not performed. The feed line appears only after the take is final; the △ line only after the press.
  • P4 Speech is the main verb; menus are the bridge. No words while talking; the slot makes the menu the place where speech lands, not a replacement for it.
  • P5 Intent in, outcome out. The gate sits exactly on rewrites, doubt and harm; everything else performs at once. The gated "do this instead?" keeps the rewrite honest: the player watches their words morph into it before it is performed.
  • P7 Bigger things, bigger effects. A heard take is just its line in the feed; a landing is a press with sparks; a reason is red and shakes; a join stays bigger than all of these.
  • P9 Timing is most of the feel. Every transition is under 320 ms except the ones that wait on the network, and those show progress (typed words, the sweep) instead of a spinner; plain talk shows nothing while it waits.
  • P11 Every model-facing word is reviewable. This proposal adds no model-facing text. The gate field is a derived flag from answers Jev already gives.

9. Open questions that remain

  1. Should the ✕/✓ strip also appear for curated menu rows, or only for slots and learned verbs? (The mockup shows it for every row, per Josh's ask; "tap the row again" makes the strip optional for fast players.)
  2. P ≥ 0.75 for the auto path is a guess from the inspire logs (ok at 0.83 was the misheard hit). It should be tuned on a week of live ev: inspire records.
  3. (Closed: talking is invisible after fix10, so there are no partial words on screen in any language.)
  4. Scene transitions as a bigger "being in the scene" beat (Josh's 18:09 note to Base Models): not in this proposal.
