B. What this proposal changes or adds, by impact

B1. The ray should run from the mic into the slot (highest visual impact, cheap)

  • Now: action_ui.rb slot_beams draws rays standing inside the slot. The connection between the thing the thumb is holding and the place the words will land is not drawn.
  • Proposal: one beam from the top of the mic to the slot's centre (two sprites/beam.png at angle, widths 26 and 9 px, blendmode_enum: 2, alpha 0.55/0.6, width and offset modulated by two sines), plus 4–8 ✦/· labels drifting along it; it thins to 35 % on release and fades 300 ms after the judge answers. Keep fix10's in-slot sheen as the beam's landing glow.
  • Where: action_ui.rb new mic_ray(from, to, level, thin) called from draw_slot10 for phases :listening :sending :judging; layout.rb mic_at for the origin; the slot's rect from menu_geo.
  • Budget: two sprites + ≤8 labels per frame. If perf_test.py --throttle 6 drops under 30 fps, draw the ray into the existing koma-style render target at half size.
  • Verify on a phone: hold the violet mic with the menu open; the ray must appear within one frame, breathe with your voice, thin on release, and be gone 300 ms after the label lands.

B2. The gate: rewrites and doubt wait; only confident, unchanged, harmless acts go by themselves

  • Now: pend sets auto_at for rewrites too (AUTO_REWRITTEN), so an "Attempt to fly" performs on its own if the player does nothing. Josh's 18:09 §9(d): "Wait for the green confirm on rewrites, doubts or low margins."
  • Proposal: auto_at = nil whenever rewritten, whenever the judge's top category P < 0.75, or whenever the margin to the second category < 0.25; AUTO_PLAIN stays for the rest but should drop to 320 ms (the proposal's landed_auto → pressed); 1.5 s is a long time to stare at a slot that will press itself.
  • Where: sidecar/inspire.py decide already returns confidence and probs (top 5); add gate: bool and margin: float to the dict so player.rb pend reads one flag instead of recomputing. server.py api_inspire passes r through unchanged, so nothing else moves. Hostile stays as harmful? in player.rb (presets hit shoot shove grab, draw, items) plus the sidecar's HOSTILE for learned verbs.
  • Verify: say "fly over to her": the slot must show the ✓/✕ strip and wait; say "wave at her": it performs by itself within half a second; say "punch her": it waits. Log line ev: inspire shows gate and margin.

B3. A reason is not final: the slot stays, the next mic press replaces it

  • Now: tick_confirm clears a :reason slot after 230 ticks (~3.8 s); inspire cancels a :confirm slot when the mic is pressed again, but a :reason slot has already vanished.
  • Proposal: a :reason slot persists until ✕, the stick, ACT, or the next mic press (which reuses the slot: inspire → @slot = @slot.merge(phase: :listening, …) instead of a new slot). Show "hold the mic to try again" at the right of the slot. The feed copy stays as is.
  • Where: player.rb tick_confirm (when :reason), inspire (the cancel_slot(:replaced) branch should cover :reason too), action_ui.rb draw_slot10 (:reason text), lang.rb (both languages).
  • Verify: "set my rose on fire" → red slot; hold the mic again without leaving the menu: the red slot turns violet and listens.

B4. The menu card: beside the target, not docked low (a disagreement; Josh decides)

  • Now: fix10.md §4 asked for the card "anchored low rather than floating by the target", and layout.rb @menu_floor does that.
  • Proposal: keep it beside the target (fix3's placement, build_menu_rows), because the thing you act on and its verbs should be one glance apart and the map must stay the performance (UX_IMPLICATIONS priority 2). The thumbs do not need to reach the card: the stick selects and ✓/✕ sit under the selected row wherever the card is.
  • Trade-off, honestly: the low card is nearer the thumbs for tapping rows and keeps the top of the map clear; the target card keeps the target in view and makes far-drag targets legible. If both exist, make it a Settings row ("Menu: by the target / near the thumbs") and test with Josh; do not keep two code paths without a switch.
  • Where: player.rb build_menu_rows (the candidate sides and keep_clear scores), layout.rb @menu_floor*.

B5. The take card rises from the mic and lifts into the feed (small deltas to the pill)

  • Now: draw_speech10 / @pill is a strip over the mic, left of ACT, and the sent line "flies" into the feed.
  • Proposal: keep it. Three details: show the partial words in a dashed box and the final in a solid bold one (the player must be able to tell "still forming" from "this is it"); pop a ✓ badge on the mic's shoulder for 300 ms at the final (the eye is on the thumb); lift the card into the feed only after 420 ms so the final can be read. Failure kinds keep their own looks: grey for didn't catch that (plus the existing ? over the @), amber for mic not ready / blocked (persisting until the next press).
  • Where: action_ui.rb draw_speech10, draw_live_card; player.rb @talk_ui phases; skin_color.rb / skin_ascii.rb draw_mic (the badge).
  • Verify: on a cold page the first press shows the grey dashed ring and "MIC WARMING"; an early release says "let go · hold again" and the next press works; a normal take shows dashed words, then bold words, then the line in the feed.

B6. The commit moment: one shared beat for menu picks and slot presses

  • Now: press_fx (white rect + glow, additive) exists; @pressed[:ticks] holds the beat.
  • Proposal: make the beat the same for a ✓ on a curated row and on a slot: white wash 420 ms, hitstop 5 ticks on the UI layer only (not the map), a · glyph ring (dot_ring, 600 ms), 9–12 glyph sparks from the row's right end through the marks system, a 2-frame 14 % flash, 3 px nudge for 180 ms, and the tick sound (tools/synth_fx.py: 1800→600 Hz, 250 ms). Then the △ line at +520 ms, the menu leaves at +900 ms (+1100 for a slot, so the new row is seen).
  • Where: player.rb confirm_choice, confirm_slot, run_confirmed, @pressed[:ticks]; action_ui.rb press_fx, dot_ring; stage.rb time_scale (a UI-only variant: freeze @pressed/slot/ray timers, not @presets); audio.rb + tools/synth_fx.py for the tick.
  • Verify: a ✓ feels like a hit (the menu freezes for a beat); the map keeps breathing underneath.

B7. Green bars over the player in both skins

  • Now: fix10.md §9(a) asks for it in colour; the ASCII skin today only has the ?.
  • Proposal: five bars (4 px wide, 4 px gap, 4–24 px by level, per-bar phase as the web build's drawVoice) with an additive glow in colour; | : . glyph columns in ASCII; a faint grey stub plus "…" while arming.
  • Where: skin_color.rb draw_talk_indicator (l.1354), skin_ascii.rb draw_talk_indicator (l.1008); pl.level from the bridge.

B8. Cancel while judging must discard the late answer

  • Now: inspired ignores a result when @slot[:phase] != :judging || @slot[:heard] != text; good. But api_inspire has already added the verb to player_actions and spread it (spread()), so a cancelled action still enters the menus later.
  • Proposal: send an inspire_id with the request; a cancel posts /api/inspire/cancel {id}; the sidecar skips pa.add and spread() for a cancelled id (or adds it with uses: 0 and no spread). Minor, but it keeps principle 10 (nothing kept that was not seen).
  • Where: server.py api_inspire (+ a small api_inspire_cancel), player.rb cancel_slot, net.rb.

B9. Timings table to adopt (ms unless ticks)

See proposal.md §4. The ones that differ from fix10's constants: AUTO_PLAIN 90 ticks → 20 ticks (320 ms); :reason auto-clear 230 ticks → never; slot open 220 ms (fix10's slot_height eases over 10 ticks = 167 ms, fine); menu stays 400 ms after a pick, 1100 ms after a slot press.

B10. The slot at the very bottom, and no struck-through original (Josh, 18:45 JST)

  • Josh: "make the inspire action at the very bottom. also no need to maintain the line" (see the proposal's changelog for how it is read).
  • Now: player.rb menu_geo already draws the slot after the visible rows, so it sits at the bottom of the visible window (@menu_top … MENU_MAX), not necessarily below every row. action_ui.rb draw_slot10 draws the heard words as a small grey subline under the churning label when s[:rewritten].
  • Proposal: while a slot is open, scroll the window so the last row and the slot are visible (@menu_top = [n - MENU_MAX, 0].max) and treat the slot as selected; on ✕, restore the previous @sel. In draw_slot10, drop the s[:rewritten] && s[:heard] subline. Instead, churn from the heard words into the label (the same churn_text motion, seeded with s[:heard] rather than blanks), for rewrites and kept labels alike. Append the learned verb at the end of @actions so it stays at the bottom once performed. Keep the ray (B1) and the feed's rewrite line.
  • Verify: with a long menu scrolled to the top, hold the mic: the slot opens below the last row and the selection jumps to it. Say "fly over to her": the words morph into "Attempt to fly" with no grey line left. ✓: "Attempt to fly" is the last row.
